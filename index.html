Tangle Trail privacy policy

Arrow Maze: Tangle Trail · Kasiga Innovations Inc · Effective October 12, 2026

Your game progress

No account is needed. Levels, results, settings and your current attempt are saved on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your puzzle history. Browser saves are separate from iPhone saves. Removing the app or clearing the browser's site data removes the corresponding local progress.

Advertising in the iPhone app

The iPhone app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertisements and privacy choices. Ads appear in the main menu, at occasional breaks between campaign levels, and when you choose an optional rewarded ad after losing all three hearts. Completing a rewarded ad can earn one extra life per attempt; a free retry is always available. The web testing game uses labeled placement previews instead of live AdMob ads.

Google's advertising services may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash and performance information. These support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service reliability. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing. We request non-personalized ads and disable publisher privacy personalization and publisher first-party identifiers. The app does not request permission to use the advertising identifier for tracking.

Where required, Google's consent form appears before ad requests. You can revisit applicable choices through Privacy choices in the main menu. If consent or an ad is unavailable, you can continue playing. Read Google's privacy policy and how Google uses advertising information for its processing, retention and controls.

Website and support

This privacy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. Its hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and browser request logs, to serve and protect the website. See GitHub's privacy policy. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use those details to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Audience and your choices

Tangle Trail is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not ask for a birth date, precise location, contacts or photos. To ask about access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights, contact info@kasigainnovations.ca. We may need enough information to identify a support request. Google handles rights requests for data it controls through its privacy tools.

Updates

We will update this page when our practices change and show the new effective date here.
